Is Apex Brokerage for businesses or individuals?

Both. The experience is designed for B2B teams and B2C private clients seeking structured, human-reviewed insurance guidance.

Does AI replace a human insurance advisor?

No. AI supports risk analysis and document review, while human advisors remain responsible for final recommendations.

Can you help clients in Germany?

Yes. The site and service positioning are Germany-focused, including support for private clients, businesses, and newcomers.

Do you support EU businesses?

Yes. Apex Brokerage is positioned for EU-oriented companies that need clear insurance comparisons and coverage gap analysis.

Is my data handled securely?

The frontend is designed around GDPR-conscious principles, minimal data collection, clear consent, and secure submission workflows.

Can startups and SMEs request insurance packages?

Yes. Startups and SMEs can request package guidance for liability, cyber, employee benefits, property, and growth-stage risks.
